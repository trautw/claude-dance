# SCDVIEWER

A 3D ballroom where a spectator walks around and watches one set of Scottish country dancers perform the reel "Mairi's Wedding" to a live band.

## The dance

**Dance**:
"Mairi's Wedding", a reel by James B. Cosh, danced 8×40 bars by 3 couples in a 4-couple longwise set.
_Avoid_: Song, routine, choreography

**Crib**:
The written, bar-by-bar description of the dance's figures; the single reference for what dancers do when.
_Avoid_: Script, notes, instructions

**Bar**:
One musical measure of the reel; the unit in which all dance timing is counted.
_Avoid_: Measure, beat, step

**Figure**:
A named, bar-bounded section of the crib (e.g. "half diagonal reel of four", "circle six hands round and back").
_Avoid_: Move, section, part

**Time through**:
One complete 40-bar pass of the crib; the dance consists of 8 of them.
_Avoid_: Round, repetition, loop, iteration

**Chord**:
The opening and closing musical flourish during which dancers bow and curtsey; not part of any time through.
_Avoid_: Intro, outro

## The set

**Set**:
The 4 couples standing in two facing lines (men's side, women's side) with the top toward the band.
_Avoid_: Group, formation, line

**Couple**:
One man and one woman dancing as partners in the set.
_Avoid_: Pair, partners

**Couple A–D**:
A couple's fixed identity for the whole dance, shown by its colour; it never changes as the set progresses.
_Avoid_: 1st couple, the 1s (as identity)

**Place**:
A couple's current standing position in the set, numbered 1–4 from the top; it changes as couples progress.
_Avoid_: Position, slot

**Dancing couple**:
The couple leading the current time through; across the dance the order is A, A, B, B, C, C, D, D.
_Avoid_: Active couple, the 1s, lead couple

**Step down**:
The swap at the end of a couple's second time leading, when they move from 3rd to 4th place and the 4th-place couple moves up.
_Avoid_: Progression, swap, rotate

**Corner**:
For a dancing couple standing between their corners, the specific diagonal neighbour they dance a figure with (1st corner, 2nd corner).
_Avoid_: Neighbour, diagonal

## Steps

**Skip change of step**:
The travelling reel step, one per bar, used whenever a dancer moves along a path.
_Avoid_: Walk, skip, travelling step

**Slip step**:
The sideways step of the circle, two to a bar: step out, close, step out, close.
_Avoid_: Side step, chassé

**First position**:
How a dancer stands when not dancing: heels together, toes turned out.
_Avoid_: Rest pose, idle, standing position

**Bow and curtsey**:
The men's bow and the women's curtsey to their partners on each chord.
_Avoid_: Greeting, reverence

## The hall

**Hall**:
The enclosed ballroom containing the dance floor, the stage, and the walls the spectator cannot pass.
_Avoid_: Room, level, scene

**Band**:
The four musicians on stage (accordion, fiddle, piano, drums) whose music the set dances to.
_Avoid_: Musicians, orchestra

**Tune**:
The original 40-bar reel melody the band plays once per time through.
_Avoid_: Song, track

**Spectator**:
The person the user controls, who walks the hall in third-person view; dancers ignore them.
_Avoid_: Player, avatar, character

**Gallery view**:
The overhead view looking down on the whole set, as a teacher on a balcony would see it.
_Avoid_: Top-down camera, overview, bird's eye

**Timeline**:
The control showing the whole dance, divided by time through and figure, used to jump to any bar.
_Avoid_: Scrubber, seek bar, progress bar
