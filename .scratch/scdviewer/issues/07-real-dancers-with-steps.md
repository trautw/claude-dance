# 07: Real dancers with steps

**What to build:** Rigged, stylised human dancers made in Blender replace the capsules, with procedural tartan and a distinct colour per couple: men in kilts and jackets, women in white dresses with tartan sashes. Blender provides step clips (skip change of step, standing in first position, bow, curtsey; ADR 0001). Skip change is locked to the bar while travelling; waiting dancers stand in first position; everyone bows and curtseys on the chords. Kilts and skirts sway.
