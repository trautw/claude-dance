# 04: Jumping in time and speed

**What to build:** The user can move to any point in the dance. A clickable, draggable timeline is marked by time through and figure. `[`/`]` step one bar, `,`/`.` jump one figure, Space pauses and resumes. Jumps land on bar boundaries. A speed selector offers 0.5×, 0.75× and 1×. Dancers are exactly where the crib puts them after any jump or speed change.
