# 09: The band on stage

**What to build:** Four Blender-made musicians sit on the stage: accordion, fiddle, piano and drums. Each plays a short loop locked to the beat (bellows, bowing, hands on the keys, sticks) and stops when the music is paused. Pressing E near the band pauses or resumes the music and the dance.

**Blocked by:** 05, 06

**Status:** done

- [x] The musicians freeze on pause
- [x] An on-screen prompt appears when the spectator is close enough to press E; pressing E pauses or resumes
